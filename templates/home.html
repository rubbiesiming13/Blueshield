About BlueShield

BlueShield is a secure web-based Police Arrest Records and Case Management System developed for the Madang Provincial Police Command.

The system is designed to improve how police records are entered, stored, managed and accessed while reducing reliance on paper-based records.

BlueShield supports police officers, station commanders, prosecution personnel and the Provincial Police Command through a centralized digital platform.

Our Purpose

To support the Madang Provincial Police Command with secure, organized and efficient digital tools for managing police records and cases.

BlueShield represents how ICT can be applied to real community needs and contribute to a more efficient digital future for Papua New Guinea.
